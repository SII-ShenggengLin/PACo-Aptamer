    Check for updates                              RNAs are critical for biological processes, and their functions are closely
                                                   tied to their three-dimensional structures. The design of RNA sequences
                                                   that fold into target three-dimensional structures is a complex challenge
                                                   due to the dynamic and unstable nature of RNA structures. Here we
                                                   present an RNA design approach, AlignIF, that leverages multiple structure
                                                   alignment with cross-graph modeling and carefully hand-designed features
                                                   to capture evolutionarily conserved structural patterns at the structural
                                                   level, facilitating RNA sequence design. AlignIF outperforms existing
                                                   state-of-the-art methods in test sets. Notably, it enables the design of
                                                   entire RNA families rather than being restricted to recapitulating native
                                                   sequences. Furthermore, AlignIF successfully designs functional RNA
                                                   fluorescent aptamers and self-cleaving ribozymes that exhibit measurable
                                                   activity without requiring any postdesign filtering. For Mango-I, two of
                                                   ten designed aptamers showed enhanced fluorescence, and two others
                                                   exhibited improved binding affinity compared with the wild type. For
                                                   the more challenging iMango-III, all designs were fluorescent, including
                                                   one with a sequence identity of ~30% relative to the wild type. The results
                                                   highlight the potential of AlignIF for functional RNA engineering.
